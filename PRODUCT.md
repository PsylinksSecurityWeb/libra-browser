## The long description

### What Libra Browser is

Libra Browser is a security-focused web browser for Windows. You use it the way you use any browser: type an address or a search, open tabs, save bookmarks, download files. The difference is what happens in the background. Libra Browser is built around three promises: **secure, private and lightweight.**

The whole app is about 3 MB. It installs in seconds, needs no administrator rights and no account, and opens in a calm white-and-grey window with nothing in the way.

### The problems it solves

**"Ads are everywhere."**
Pages jump around, pop-ups cover the text, videos start by themselves. Libra Browser stops ad and tracker servers before they load, and tidies the empty gaps they leave. Pages open faster and use less data.

**"Websites follow me around."**
You look at one product and see it advertised for weeks. Libra Browser blocks trackers, and it also changes the small details that sites use to recognise your device (a "fingerprint"), differently for every site and every time you open the browser.

**"I don't know who has my data."**
Most big browsers ask you to sign in and sync your life to their servers. Libra Browser has no sign-in and no sync. What it stores stays on your PC, encrypted to your Windows account. One page shows what is stored and clears it with a button.

**"My computer is slow."**
Libra Browser is tiny. Tabs you are not using go to sleep within seconds and wake when you click them. After a long time unused, Libra Browser asks if you want to close a tab. It adapts on older PCs.

**"Downloads are slow and break."**
Big files download in several pieces at once. If a download breaks, Retry carries on where it stopped. You see live speed and progress, and a small card slides in when a download starts and finishes.

**"I worry about my children online."**
Child Safety mode blocks adult, unsafe and scam sites and keeps ad blocking on. It can only be turned on or off with your Windows password, PIN or fingerprint, and it blocks private windows and developer tools. A parent can unlock one site for 30 minutes.

**"I reuse weak passwords."**
Libra Browser has a built-in password maker (up to 286 characters) and an optional saved-passwords page. Showing or copying a saved password asks for your Windows sign-in first.

**"Some sites break with an ad blocker."**
Each site has three levels: Off, Balanced or Max. Pick what that site needs and Libra Browser remembers. Government, banking and tool sites that need to see everything work with Off.

**"Browsers are complicated."**
A short first start, plain words, thin flat design, no clutter, no shopping offers, no news feed.

## Features

### Blocks ads and trackers (Cloud ad blocking)
- Ads and trackers are blocked by name, in the cloud, before they reach your PC.
- **Three levels per site.** Off lets everything through. Balanced blocks all trackers and lets about half of the ad networks load, a different half each time. Max blocks all of it (default).
- A shield button next to the address bar shows what was blocked on the page: ads, trackers, analytics, social trackers, and the top blocked servers.
- A Privacy page shows a 7-day report of what Libra Browser stopped.
- Empty ad gaps are removed so pages do not look broken.

### Private by design
- No account, no sign-in, no sync, no tracking. Optional, clearly labelled sponsored links and a sponsored picture can appear on the new-tab page. They are chosen on your PC, nothing about you is sent, and one click in Settings turns them off.
- Fingerprint protection: canvas, graphics card, plugins, screen and device details are changed or hidden per site.
- Secure sites first: Libra Browser opens the https version when there is one and warns before using an insecure page.
- Optional third-party cookie blocking.
- Camera, microphone and location always ask first. Motion sensors are blocked, except on sites you set to Off.
- Private windows leave no history, cookies or site data.
- One Privacy page: see what is stored and clear the last hour, day, week or everything.

### Protects your PC
- Pop-ups and downloads you did not ask for are blocked.
- A risky file (programs and scripts) shows a "This file can run programs" card when it finishes: Keep or Delete.
- Internal engine pages, local-file pages and developer tools cannot be opened by websites.
- History, saved sites, bookmarks and other lists are stored encrypted with your Windows account.
- Libra Browser's local network helper accepts connections only from Libra Browser itself and refuses dangerous ports and tricks that would skip the filters.

### Child Safety mode
- One switch in Settings > Family.
- Blocks adult, malware, phishing, proxy and VPN sites, and keeps ads and trackers blocked.
- Turning it on or off needs the Windows sign-in. Uninstalling Libra Browser asks for it too, and the setting survives a reinstall.
- Blocks private windows and developer tools, and Off is not available for ad blocking.
- A parent can unlock one site for 30 minutes.
- Honest note: for the strongest protection give your child a standard Windows account. Anyone with administrator rights can remove any app's lock.

### Fast and light
- About 3 MB, starts quickly, sleeps unused tabs, and uses fewer resources on older PCs.
- Fast multi-connection downloads with resume.
- Optional "Save video" icon and right-click Save for images, links, videos and PDFs.
- Search suggestions from your chosen engine, fetched without cookies and never in private windows.

### Passwords
- Strong password maker with your choice of characters, length from 1 to 286, look-alike and repeat filters.
- Libra Browser always asks before saving a login: Save, Not now or Never for this site.
- Stored only on your PC and locked to your Windows account. One tap fills a login on the exact site it was saved for.
- Showing or copying a saved password needs your Windows sign-in.

### AI Assistant and Auto suggest (beta, optional)
- An assistant panel that fills forms from your own notes and reads pages for you. Bring your own API key. It asks before anything risky and never types passwords or card numbers.
- When the assistant is closed, the AI sees nothing and nothing is sent to it.
- Auto suggest (no AI, this PC only): click an empty name, e-mail, phone or company box and pick the matching value from your notes. It opens only after a real click or Tab and never for passwords, user names, cards or ID numbers.

### Everyday browser
- Tabs, pinned tabs, tab manager, restore tabs on startup.
- Bookmarks, history, downloads and settings pages in one calm style.
- Home page with editable tiles of your favourite sites.
- Six search engines (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave, Startpage, Ecosia) and two AI assistants (Perplexity, Brave Ask).
- Full screen, keyboard shortcuts, light mode.
- Set as default browser from inside Libra Browser (Windows asks you to confirm).
- One small installer, per user, no administrator rights, easy to remove.

## The honest part

- Libra Browser is built on the Microsoft WebView2 engine, the same base as Edge. Windows may still send some basic system data to Microsoft. We reduce this but cannot fully switch it off.
- Cloud ad blocking works through our DNS service (dns.psylinks-beta.com), which answers website-name lookups. The service keeps no record of what you look up. Cloudflare, which hosts it, may keep standard technical logs.
- Libra Browser is not a VPN or Tor. Your internet provider can still see which servers you connect to.
- In Child Safety mode, site names are also checked with a public family service (CleanBrowsing) until Libra Browser's own family list is ready. Gambling sites are not covered yet.
- Windows only. No phone version, no extensions and no import from other browsers yet.
- The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show a "publisher unknown" warning. Check the SHA-256 listed on the download page.
- The source code is open source under Apache-2.0. Preserve the LICENSE, NOTICE, and required attribution information.
- Some strict CAPTCHAs or sign-in pages may need Balanced or Off for that site.
